## 🔭 About Me

I'm **Amir Masoud (Masoud) Nourollah**, a PhD student at **Cardiff University**, working across the **Cardiff NLP** group and the **Knowledge Representation and Reasoning (KRR)** group in the School of Computer Science and Informatics.

- 🔬 My PhD research focuses on **explainability and interpretability of deep learning models** for **molecular dynamics** and **machine-learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs)**.
- 🧠 My MSc research applied **deep learning to neural image segmentation** — detecting neurons and quantifying their morphology in fluorescence microscopy images.
- 💼 Before academia, I worked as a **software and machine learning engineer**, building systems across computer vision, speech, and NLP.
- 🌍 Outside research, I enjoy traveling, cooking, and exploring new corners of Linux and open-source tooling.
